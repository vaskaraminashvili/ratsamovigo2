# Marketing on Facebook: რა წამოვიღო

**Evidence status:** no buyer panel, offer file or CFO numbers exist. The audience below is inferred, not proven. Facebook size comes from [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/stats/facebook-users-in-georgia/2025/12/): about 3.9 million Facebook users in Georgia (Dec 2025), 53 percent women, with ages 25 to 34 the largest group at about 1.13 million. These are Meta's estimates and not all are unique people. I found no Georgia-specific ad costs, so every cost figure below is a placeholder to replace after a small test.

## 1. First audience (hypothesis)
Household planners aged 25 to 44 who shop for a family, cook at home and already use Viber or WhatsApp groups. Parents and couples sharing groceries first. Validate with 10 interviews before spending.

## 2. Positioning (three versions)
1. For families who shop together, who keep asking "what do we need?", რა წამოვიღო is the Georgian shared list that remembers your dishes, unlike a Viber chat.
2. For people who plan dinner for the week, who forget half the ingredients, it is the list that turns dishes into one shopping list. **Recommended** (it matches Pro and the AnyList-level gap).
3. For couples splitting errands, it is the list both of you can tick off in real time.

Evidence is the competitor gap only (no Georgian list with dishes found). Change after interviews.

## 3. Channels (start with three)
| Channel | Why | Cost | How you know |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook Page + Reels | Free to start, Georgian demo videos of "add dish, list done" | time | follows, saves, link clicks |
| Facebook groups (parenting, recipes, family, cooking) | Where the household planner already is | time | installs from group posts. Ask admins first; no spam |
| Meta ads to Georgian-language audience, app install or landing page | Fast tests of hooks and price | start small, say 300 GEL | cost per install, trial start |
| Invite loop (QR, share link) | Free growth inside households | dev time | invites sent per user |

**Not doing yet:** influencers (until a hook works), Instagram/TikTok (reuse the same videos later), Google Search (low volume for this term), print.

## 5. Ten hooks (Georgian lines, English gloss)
1. "ისევ დაგავიწყდა რძე?" (Forgot the milk again?) for the forgetful shopper
2. "ერთი სია, მთელი ოჯახისთვის." (One list for the whole family.)
3. "კერძი აირჩიე, ინგრედიენტები თვითონ დაემატება." (Pick a dish, the ingredients add themselves.)
4. "Viber-ის ჩატში სია იკარგება?" (Does your list get lost in the Viber chat?) answers "my family uses Viber"
5. "ეს 6 ინგრედიენტი უკვე სიაშია." (These 6 ingredients are already on the list.) shows duplicate skipping
6. "დაგეგმე კვირა, იყიდე ერთხელ." (Plan the week, shop once.)
7. "ის რაც უკვე გაქვს, სიაში აღარ ჩაიწერება." (What you already have won't be added.)
8. "ორივემ ერთდროულად ნახე, ვინ იყიდა." (Both see who bought what, live.)
9. "14 დღე უფასოდ, ბარათის გარეშე." (14 days free, no card.) answers "why pay"
10. "პირველი 100 ოჯახი: 19 ლარი წელიწადში." (First 100 families: 19 GEL for the year.) with end date

## 6. Budget and the most you can pay (assumptions)
- First month: **300 GEL** ads (placeholder) + your time. Split 70 percent testing hooks, 30 percent price test. Everything else is organic.
- Revenue per paying household: 29 GEL in year one (plan from `pricing.md`). Start by capping acquisition cost at **15 GEL per paying household** (about half of year-one revenue). Without CFO numbers (fees, hosting) this is a cautious placeholder, not a calculation.
- If trial-to-paid is 4 percent (assumed), the cost per trial start must be at most **0.60 GEL**. Test whether that is realistic in Georgia; if not, rely on groups and the invite loop.

| Number | Change something if |

## 8. Rules
- No fake reviews, follower counts or testimonials; beta quotes only with consent.
- Do not post in groups without admin permission; follow each group's rules.
- Check Meta's ad policies for claims and Georgian advertising rules. Not legal advice.
